# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual modpack players who want to find, install, and launch Minecraft modpacks with minimal friction — plus experienced modders managing multiple instances, server hosts, and content creators who need quick instance switching. All share the same goal: play Minecraft modded, fast.

## Product Purpose

An optimized, high-quality Minecraft launcher for everyone to play Minecraft. Built as a cracked launcher but with premium features — comparable to LunarClient or FeatherClient launchers — so users get one-click modpack installation, instance management, and social/connected launching without paying for a client mod.

## Positioning

An Optimized and High Quality launcher for everyone to play Minecraft, made as a Cracked Launcher but with Premium features, just like LunarClient or FeatherClient launchers.

## Brand Personality

fast, premium, bold

## Anti-references

Generic, Sloppy, No Design Direction, Not being Consistent, AI Slop

## Design Principles

1. **One-click modpack installation with premium polish.** Fast, premium, bold — every screen should feel deliberate and high-end, never generic or rushed.
2. **Consistency over novelty.** A single coherent design language across all views, modals, and components. No sloppy inconsistencies, no drifting visual direction.
3. **Premium features for everyone.** The cracked-launcher accessibility model means no paywalls on core features; premium feel comes from execution, not gating.
4. **Accessibility without compromise.** WCAG AAA contrast, focus states, reduced motion, and screen-reader support are non-negotiable on every surface.
5. **No AI slop.** Every color, radius, spacing, and layout decision is intentional. No gradient text, no side-stripe borders, no glassmorphism as default, no eyebrow kickers on every section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA compliance across all surfaces. Reduced motion preferences respected. Focus-visible outlines on all interactive elements. Screen-reader support via sr-only content and ARIA live regions. High contrast mode support.
